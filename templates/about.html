Fair play

Results you can trust

Serious players in Bangladesh and South Asia often compete through scattered group chats, manual brackets and screenshots as proof. {{ brand.name }} gives that scene a clearer path.

  • Rules on every event

    Entry fees, eligibility and prize terms are published on each tournament page before you join.

  • Proof and review

    Scores are submitted in the match room with evidence. Disputed results go to a review queue.

  • Recorded decisions

    Sanctions and disputes are logged, so decisions stay traceable instead of getting lost in chat.

Active, and still growing

{{ brand.name }} is live and improving. Some deeper features, such as automated game verification and richer dispute tools, are still being built. We list what works today and say so when something is not ready.
