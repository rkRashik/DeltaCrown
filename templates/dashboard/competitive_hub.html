Your competitive map

DeltaCrown has four paths. Choose the one that matches what you want to do.

Showdown

Challenge another team.

Use Showdown when your team wants a direct competitive match. You can open a challenge for any eligible opponent or target a specific team.

CreateCaptain sets game, format, entry fee, and opponent.Match RoomTeams get the room for match details.ResultOne side submits the winner and score.ReviewThe opponent confirms, or the case goes under review.SettledConfirmed result and reward state are closed.

Missions

Solo goals for progression.

Use Missions when you want individual objectives. Start, complete, then submit proof if required.

StartPick a daily, weekly, or special mission.ProgressFollow the objective and deadline.ProofAdd link/note/file when proof is required.ReviewStaff checks the submission.RewardApproved mission outcome visible in feed.

Bounty

Post a challenge for others to claim.

A team posts a Bounty on itself. Other teams claim it, play the challenge, and wait for verification.

PostTeam announces the Bounty.ClaimOpponent accepts from Bounty board.Match RoomLinked room for proof and result.VerifyStaff checks the claim.RewardApproved claims settle safely.

Dropzone

Reserve a battle royale slot.

Use Dropzone for large custom lobbies. Reserve, wait for room reveal, play, then check scoring.

ReserveChoose lobby and secure slot.RevealRoom credentials appear when allowed.LobbyJoin the custom room.ScoringOperators record results.ResultsLeaderboard after scoring complete.
